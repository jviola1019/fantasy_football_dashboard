## Ablation — is the input signal worthless, or is the transform?

A single Brier number cannot separate these, and they have opposite
fixes. `draft capital` is the raw market signal (sum of totalPicks -
pick_no) BEFORE replacement level and the VBD transform touch it.
Compared on AUC, which needs no calibration — only ranking.

  AUC — shipped chain (trueValue -> sim) ... 0.3058  CI [0.1333, 0.4450]
  AUC — raw draft capital ................. 0.5033  CI [0.4850, 0.5333]
  AUC — early QB/TE share (scarcity probe)  0.6725

  READ: the raw signal ranks better than chance and the shipped chain
  ranks worse. The TRANSFORM is destroying signal, not the input.

  MECHANISM. rank-ratio VBD computes 50 + 50*(replacement - rank)/replacement,
  so the advantage at a given positional rank GROWS with position depth.
  In a 10-team league replacement is QB 12 / TE 12 but RB 35 / WR 37, so:

      QB3 -> 87.5     RB3 -> 95.7
      QB5 -> 79.2     RB5 -> 92.9

  The transform therefore systematically UNDER-values scarce positions.
  In this sample early QB/TE spend correlates with qualifying (AUC above),
  so the model marks down exactly the teams that went on to succeed. That
  is a coherent explanation for an inverted AUC, and it points at the VBD
  transform — rank ratios are not points above replacement — rather than
  at replacement level or the simulator.

## Verdict

  FAILED. The model is significantly WORSE than a constant forecast on
  this sample, and its ranking is inverted — teams it rates highly
  qualified LESS often than teams it rated poorly.

  This is a finding, not a crash. It means the shipped playoff odds
  carry no demonstrated predictive value and must not be presented as
  though they do (CLAUDE.md: 'if a model lacks calibration, do not
  present it as production-safe').

  NOT DONE, deliberately: no parameter was flipped or refitted to
  improve these numbers. With 5 effective clusters that would be
  curve-fitting to noise, and inverting a model because it scored
  badly on 5 leagues is how you turn a small sample into a big one.

  Effective sample size is nearer 5 leagues than 50 rows.
  No parameter was fitted on this data; it is a pure holdout.
